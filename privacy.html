SoloCustody Log — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

Your records never leave your phone.

SoloCustody Log is designed so that we cannot see your information — because it is never sent to us.

No accounts

There is no sign-up, no login, and no user account.

No data collection

We do not collect, receive, transmit, store on any server, sell, or share any personal information. The app contains no analytics, no advertising trackers, and no third-party tracking SDKs.

Everything is stored only on your device

Your children's first names, your custody schedule, your log entries, your expenses, and any photos you attach are stored in the app's private storage on your phone only.

Photos

Photos you attach are copied into the app's on-device storage and are never uploaded anywhere.

Exports are yours

When you create a PDF, CSV, or backup file, the app hands the file to the share sheet or files app that YOU choose. Where you send it is up to you; the app itself sends nothing.

App lock

The optional PIN and biometric lock protect the app on your device. We cannot recover your PIN, and we cannot recover your data if you lose your device without a backup export.

Children's information

Names and details about children are entered by the parent or guardian using the app and remain on that person's device. We never receive them.

Changes

If a future version changes any of this, the updated policy will appear in the app before the change takes effect.
