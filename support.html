SoloCustody Log — Support

SoloCustody Log is a private custody and parenting-time log. Everything you enter is stored only on your device — there is no account and no server copy.

Common questions

I forgot my PIN. Can you recover my data?

No — and that is by design. The PIN locks the app on your device only. We never receive your PIN or your data, so we cannot unlock the app or recover records. If you exported a backup file earlier, you can restore from that backup after reinstalling.

How do I move my records to a new phone?

On your old phone, use the Export section to create a full backup file and save it somewhere safe (for example, your Files app or a cloud folder you control). On the new phone, install SoloCustody Log and restore from that backup file.

How do I share a report?

Use the Export section to generate a PDF report of your log, then share it with your attorney or mediator using the share options on your device. The app itself sends nothing anywhere.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are handled by Apple. Open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and select SoloCustody Log to change or cancel. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

Will the other parent be notified?

No. The app never contacts the other parent or anyone else.
