SoloCustody Log

A private custody and parenting-time log for one parent. Your records stay on your phone — no account, no server, no data collected.

Download on the App Store
  • Log an exchange in seconds: late pickups, no-shows, denied visits, schedule changes, expenses, communications
  • Custody calendar with standard patterns and planned vs. actual parenting time
  • Track shared expenses and what is owed
  • Export a clean PDF record to share with your attorney or mediator
  • Private by design — no account, no sign-in, the other parent is never contacted
